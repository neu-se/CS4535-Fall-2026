## Review Defense 1

<div style={{fontSize: '0.75em', textAlign: 'left'}}>

Laptops closed. No agent. Jon picks one of your reviews and puts the thread up.

In about two minutes:

1. **What change did you ask for?**
2. **Why does it matter to the product,** to a user or to the next maintainer?
3. **How do you know the problem is real?**

Then one question from the author or the room.

</div>

<aside className="notes">
**About 30 minutes for 13 students.** Before class, open each student's review list and pick one review per student, at random from those that request a change, and don't announce the picks in advance. Code Review 1 reviews can be picked too.

**What passes:** the student can say what they asked for, why it matters to someone who uses or maintains Pawtograder, and what they did to know it was real, all without the laptop. Saying "the agent found it, and I checked it by running X" passes. Saying "the agent said so" doesn't.

**If they can't defend it,** that review doesn't count toward the six or toward Credit. Say so privately afterwards. Write it down for the checkpoint judgment.

**Anyone with no requested change** has failed the defense. Tell them after class: They write one before Dec 7 and defend it then ([code review handout](/docs/assignments/code-review#review-defense)).

**Anyone absent** defends a review you pick in office hours within a week. An unexcused absence also counts double, because this is an integration session.
