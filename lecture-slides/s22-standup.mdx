## The Post: by 10:30, in your team's channel

<div style={{fontSize: '0.75em', textAlign: 'left'}}>

- **Done:** what you finished since the last standup, with links
- **Blocked:** what's in your way, and *the specific thing you need from someone*. "Nothing" is fine if it's true
- **Next:** what you'll do before the next standup
- **Today:** if an assigned review hasn't arrived, say so in your post

The post is your attendance. "Still working on it" doesn't meet the format: a teammate who reads it should know what you did and what you need.

</div>

<aside className="notes">
Same three questions as the async sprint. Scan every post for a missing review. Each one is either a reviewer to chase today or a PR that meets the requirement on the date it was ready.

## Integration Session I

<div style={{fontSize: '0.75em', textAlign: 'left'}}>

Everyone in the room, failures on screen:

- **Shared schema:** two migrations touching the same table, or numbered to collide
- **Flags:** one team's flag changes what another team's feature sees
- **Shared files:** stacks that will conflict on merge or rebase

Leave with each collision either resolved or assigned an owner and a date, written on the issue.

</div>

<aside className="notes">

**First priority: cross-team merge conflicts.** When a first-ticket stack can't merge by Wednesday because of another team's stack, get both owners at the same screen, agree on the merge order, and write it on both issues. A stack that has to shrink to merge gets its scoped-down version agreed on the issue now, in writing.

**Second priority: review disagreements.** Review threads where the author disagrees with the reviewer come next. Disagreement backed by evidence is the goal, so help them make the argument without settling it for them.
