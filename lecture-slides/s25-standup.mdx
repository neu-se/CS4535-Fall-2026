## The Post: by 10:30, in your team's channel

<div style={{fontSize: '0.75em', textAlign: 'left'}}>

- **Done:** what you finished since the last standup, with links
- **Blocked:** what's in your way, and *the specific thing you need from someone*. "Nothing" is fine if it's true
- **Next:** what you'll do before the next standup
- **This week:** link the board issues you'll close before Nov 23

The post is your attendance. "Still working on it" doesn't meet the format: A teammate who reads it should know what you did and what you need.

</div>

<aside className="notes">
Same three questions as the async sprint. Read the posts against each team's board. An issue that's "next" in every standup since Oct 26 is a cut to discuss in clinic.
