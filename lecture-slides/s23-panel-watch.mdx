## While You Watch

<div style={{fontSize: '0.75em', textAlign: 'left'}}>

[The worksheet](pathname:///worksheets/panel-watch.html) has a row for each of the panel's four questions, and a fifth for where the panel disagrees.

- **Middle column:** a claim a panelist makes, with their name
- **Right column:** test it against your semester: your first ticket, a review, Game Day 1, or R11 and R12
- **At the bottom:** one question you'd ask the panel

Hand it in before you leave.

</div>

<aside className="notes">
Hand the worksheets out during reminders so nobody is reading the sheet when the stream starts.

The right column is the part that matters. A sheet that copies down what panelists said with nothing on the right has missed the task. Row 5 is the hardest one. A student who finds a disagreement between panelists, or a claim that R11 or R12 contradicts, was listening closely.

The panel ends at 11:30, which leaves five minutes to collect worksheets before class ends at 11:35. If the Q&A has a virtual channel, students can send the question from the bottom box during the Q&A.
