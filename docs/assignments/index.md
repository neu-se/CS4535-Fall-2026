## How work is assessed

Nearly everything in this course is graded from **natural artifacts**, the things professional software work produces anyway. Pull requests, reviews, commit history, design docs, research notes, Discord threads, monitoring dashboards. There are very few artificial submissions.

That means *when* and *how* you work is visible, and it counts. Cadence, surfacing blockers early, and responding to review are assessed alongside what you shipped.

Each handout therefore ends the same way: an **expectations table**, then what strong and weak versions of the work actually look like. Read those two sections before you start, not after you finish. They are the rubric.
